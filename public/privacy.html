Privacy

This site collects as little as possible. Here is everything it does.

The contact form

When you send the form on the contact page, your name, phone, email and message go to Mama Africa so the restaurant can answer you — for catering quotes, hours questions, orders. Messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and keep records of orders. Phone and email are optional; leaving them off is fine, but it makes it harder to answer.

No cookies, no trackers

This site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or analytics scripts. Nothing is counted, profiled or sold.

The map

The contact page shows a Google Maps embed for directions. When it loads, Google receives standard request data such as your IP address, under Google's own privacy terms.

Your choices

Want your message record deleted, or have a question about any of this? Email ventica64@gmail.com or call (515) 735-1141 and say so.
