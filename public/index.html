From the market to Main Street

“She started cooking her family recipes as a coping mechanism.”

Ventica Woods was born in Liberia and fled the civil war at 14, landing in Des Moines. The dishes she cooked to feel at home became a business: first meals sold from her kitchen, then catering, weddings, community events, festivals and the Downtown Farmers’ Market — where diners kept running into her jollof. On Aug. 1, 2026, Mama Africa opened its first permanent home inside the Local Bites food hall.

Story as told to the Des Moines Register and Hola America News

Good questions

Before you come

Where exactly is Mama Africa?

Inside the Local Bites food hall on the lower level of the Greater Des Moines Partnership building at 700 Locust St, downtown Des Moines — next to the Marriott, reachable through the skywalk.

What time is lunch?

Local Bites advertises weekday lunch from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., Monday through Friday. Evening and weekend service varies — call (515) 735-1141.

What is jollof rice?

A tomato-based rice dish cooked with onions and spices, beloved across West Africa. Here it comes as a lunch base for bowls and wraps, and on the menu beside fufu, egusi and plantains.

Can I just grab takeout?

Yes — Local Bites offers carryout alongside its shared seating.

I want Mama Africa to cater my event.

Great. Call (515) 735-1141 or send the contact form with your date and headcount, and ask about menus and availability.
