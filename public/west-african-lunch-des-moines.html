Three points at the counter

How the bowl comes together

1. Base

Jollof rice — tomatoes, onions and spices cooked into the rice — or white rice, jollof noodles, or fried cabbage.

2. Protein

Grilled beef or grilled chicken.

3. Toppings

Sweet plantains, red beans, steamed onions or peppers, corn or tomatoes. Ask for a wrap instead of a bowl.

Lunch questions

Asked at the counter

What time is lunch?

Local Bites advertises weekday lunch from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., Monday through Friday. Call (515) 735-1141 to confirm before your visit.

Is lunch the same every day?

The build-your-own format is the steady one; the day’s boards decide the rest. Call or check the Facebook page for the latest.

I have an office order for a dozen. Can you handle it?

Call (515) 735-1141 with your headcount and time — and if it’s bigger than a lunch run, the catering page is where that conversation really starts.

What about dinner?

Egusi with fufu or rice, fufu with okra sauce, potato greens with rice and more — evenings, hours vary. See the full menu.
